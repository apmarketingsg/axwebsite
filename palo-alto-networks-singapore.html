Different controls solve different problems

Plan firewall security and upstream DDoS protection together.

A firewall policy governs the traffic your network accepts. A large volumetric attack can also overwhelm connectivity before traffic reaches that firewall. Review upstream DDoS protection alongside the firewall design where keeping public-facing services reachable is important.

Axclusive offers optional Always-On Cloudflare Magic Transit protection for its DIA customers. Discuss the protected service and agreed scope rather than assuming firewall subscriptions include upstream attack mitigation.

Yes. Share the environment and required deployment model. Hardware and VM-Series options need different sizing, compatibility and licensing checks; the suitable product and scope are confirmed for the project.

No. Features and security services depend on the platform, software and subscriptions selected. Review the exact entitlements and support term in the quotation.

No. Ongoing Axclusive management and upstream DDoS protection are separate services. Discuss these requirements so the proposal identifies the appropriate service and responsibility.

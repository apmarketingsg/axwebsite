What is Cloud Connect?

A private connection to the cloud environments your business uses.

Private cloud connectivity links your network to supported cloud services without sending the agreed cloud traffic over public Internet routes. It can help you plan performance and capacity for applications that span your own infrastructure and the cloud.

A retail team needs its cloud application to work during the busiest trading hour. A hosting provider needs to move customer data into a cloud environment. A business needs its overnight backup to finish before staff arrive. Start with the work and the deadlines; we’ll help plan the connection behind them.

Capacity and costs

Size access and cloud capacity together. Axclusive’s MSSC access supports 1G–100G options; the speed available for an individual cloud connection depends on the cloud and delivery design. Confirm circuit, equipment, cloud-port and data-transfer charges separately.

Backup and continuity

Ask about dual access circuits and the cloud-side resilience you need. Review shared cables, equipment, building entries and cloud connections, then agree how failover will be tested.

Security and management

Private connectivity does not automatically encrypt data. Agree encryption and firewall needs, plus who manages routing and cloud-account configuration. Ongoing managed service can be scoped where required.

Cloud Connectivity FAQ

How is private cloud connectivity different from Internet access?+
Internet access reaches public websites and services. A private cloud connection carries the agreed traffic to supported cloud resources through a private path. You may need both; cloud connectivity does not replace all Internet access.
Which clouds can Axclusive help me connect to?+
Discuss AWS Direct Connect, Azure ExpressRoute and Google Cloud Interconnect requirements with us. We confirm the supported region, connection type and delivery arrangement for your site and workload.
Can I connect an office as well as a data centre?+
Yes, share the office or data centre address. Access availability, installation and the path to your chosen cloud are confirmed for the proposed service.
Can I connect to more than one cloud?+
Share each provider, region and workload. We can assess the required connections and how they fit your access design. Connecting to several clouds does not automatically include routing directly between those clouds; that needs separate assessment.
Does Cloud Connect include encryption or make every SaaS application private?+
No. Encryption and supported destinations must be specified. Do not assume every Microsoft 365 service or other SaaS application is reachable through a private cloud connection.
Can I combine cloud access with Internet and private Ethernet?+
Yes. MSSC combines the agreed services on a common access circuit. Choose one physical port or separate service ports through CPE. Dual circuits can be requested for resilience.
What information do you need for a quote?+
Your site address or data centre, cloud provider and region, required capacity, applications, backup needs and target date. Include any existing equipment or cloud connections so we can assess the complete design.

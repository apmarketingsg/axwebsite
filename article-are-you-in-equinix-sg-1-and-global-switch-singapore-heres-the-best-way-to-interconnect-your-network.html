Are You in Equinix SG1 and Global Switch Singapore? Here’s the Best Way to Interconnect Your Network

May 28, 2025 · Axclusive · Updated 17 November 2026

When equipment is split between Equinix SG1 and Global Switch Singapore, the inter-site connection becomes part of the application design. Axclusive can assess private connectivity between the agreed facilities, subject to availability, access and cross-connect requirements.

Match the handoff to the workload

For Ethernet services, confirm the required port speed, committed capacity, MTU and VLAN arrangement. Where optical transport is required, confirm the specific client interface and equipment compatibility in the proposal. Do not assume every legacy storage, OTN or Ethernet interface is available on every service.

Optical transport still needs engineering

A fibre or DWDM link can suffer attenuation, degraded signal quality, errors or equipment failures. It is not simply a binary working-or-failed system. Monitoring, optical budgets and fault isolation remain important. Dedicated capacity does not eliminate propagation delay or guarantee every application outcome.

Choose DCI or a managed optical network

Data Center Interconnect suits an agreed service between data-centre endpoints. Managed Optical Fiber Network includes the agreed dark fibre, DWDM optical equipment and managed-service scope. Protected or unprotected designs should be chosen against the application’s tolerance for an outage.

Confirm what is included

  • Exact buildings, meet-me rooms, demarcation points and cross-connect responsibilities.
  • Port and service bandwidth: available options are assessed within the published 1G to 100G connectivity range.
  • Physical diversity, equipment redundancy and failover behaviour.
  • Monitoring, support, SLA boundaries, charges and realistic activation dates.

Discuss the sites you need to connect

Hosting providers, carriers and enterprises can share their facility names, racks, traffic requirements and target date. Ask Axclusive to assess the interconnect.

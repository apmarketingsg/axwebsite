Are You in Equinix SG1 and Global Switch Singapore? Here’s the Best Way to Interconnect Your Network

May 28, 2025

In this article
Browse all blog ↗

If your infrastructure spans Equinix SG1 and Global Switch Singapore, you know the challenges of interconnecting two major data centers. We've been there - and we’ve solved it.

Our interconnect solution is based on dedicated optical wavelengths, not traditional Layer 2 or Layer 3 handoffs. Why? Because we’ve experienced firsthand the pain of debugging black-box provider networks.

Why Wavelength Matters

In the past, we used carrier-provided Layer 2 and Layer 3 services. But when issues arose—packet loss, flapping interfaces, unpredictable latency, or software bugs introduced during provider upgrades - it became a nightmare to troubleshoot. With multiple hidden layers and limited visibility, resolving problems was slow, painful, and costly.

That’s why we invested in a DWDM-based solution. With wavelengths:

  • It either works or it doesn’t - no guesswork, no black-box routing.
  • You get full transparency and end-to-end control.
  • Low latency and high throughput are guaranteed.
  • Troubleshooting is straightforward and fast.
What We Offer
  • Client-Side Optical Interfaces
    We support a wide range of client-side optical ports to meet diverse connectivity needs:
    • Ethernet: 1G, 10G, 25G, 40G, 100G
    • OTN (Optical Transport Network): OTU2, OTU2e, OTU4
    • SDH/SONET: STM-64, OC-192
    • Storage Area Network (SAN): FC100, FC200, FC400, FC800, FICON8G, FC1200, FC1600, FC3200
  • C-band Wavelength Services
    Rent dedicated C-band wavelengths for high-capacity, low-latency links.
  • DWDM Equipment Rental or Purchase
    Need to aggregate multiple services over a single wavelength? We offer DWDM gear to help you scale efficiently - available for rent or purchase.
  • Pre-Provisioned Capacity
    Accelerate your deployment with our ready-to-activate infrastructure.
  • End-to-End Support
    Get expert assistance and monitoring from the same team that built and operates the platform.
  • Simple, Transparent Pricing
    No hidden fees or surprises - just straightforward pricing and clear deliverables.
Why Choose Us?
  • Flexible Bandwidth Options: Choose from 1G to 100G ports—perfect for low-latency applications and high-throughput requirements.
  • Turnkey DWDM Solutions: No need to spend months procuring your own DWDM gear. We offer rental or sales options on tested and supported optical equipment at attractive pricing.
  • Fast Deployment: Our team understands the urgency of getting your circuits up. We help you go live faster with pre-provisioned capacity and local support.
  • Cross Connects Simplified: We handle the logistics and coordination across both Equinix and Global Switch data centers so you don’t have to.
Who Benefits
  • Cloud and SaaS providers who demand low-latency links
  • Enterprises seeking high-speed east-west traffic flow
  • ISPs and carriers needing scalable, dedicated paths
  • Fintech or AI firms requiring consistent and predictable bandwidth

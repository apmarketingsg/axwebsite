Practical answers

Before you choose

Can I buy Fortinet products without Managed SD-WAN?+
Yes. Discuss product supply and the subscriptions you require. Deployment, ongoing management and connectivity are separate scopes that can be included in an agreed proposal.
What information helps size a FortiGate firewall?+
Share your expected traffic, users, sessions, inspection requirements, interfaces and availability needs. Confirm capacity for the enabled features rather than relying only on interface speed.
Can you quote a Fortinet subscription renewal?+
Send the product details, current services and renewal date through the agreed enquiry process. We will confirm the applicable subscription and support options for your equipment.
Is Fortinet support the same as Axclusive managed support?+
No. Vendor support and subscription entitlements are distinct from Axclusive monitoring, configuration changes and operational support. Each responsibility is confirmed in the proposal.

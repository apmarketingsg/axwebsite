What is Magic Transit?

Protect the network before the flood reaches it.

Cloudflare Magic Transit is a network security service that protects routed IP infrastructure against DDoS attacks. Axclusive helps you assess how it fits your connectivity and how protected traffic reaches your services.

Start with the public systems that cannot afford to disappear: hosted applications, customer networks, business services or infrastructure across data centres. Protection should be designed around those dependencies.

Look beyond the checkbox

ISP DDoS protection vs Cloudflare Magic Transit: what changes?

“DDoS protection included” does not tell you how much attack traffic a service can handle, when protection starts or what happens when its limits are reached. Compare the actual service design.

ISP services vary: some also use large-scale cloud mitigation. Compare the specified capacity, routing and incident policy rather than the provider label. Cloudflare’s aggregate platform capacity is not dedicated bandwidth reserved for one customer.

Internet speed ≠ attack capacity

A 1 Gbps connection can face a much larger attack.

Imagine a 1 Gbps internet service with a 10 Gbps mitigation limit. If an attack exceeds that limit, the provider may blackhole the targeted IP to protect its wider network, depending on its policy.

That can stop the flood from affecting other customers, but it also drops legitimate traffic to the blackholed destination. Your circuit may still be up while customers cannot reach your service.

This is an illustrative scenario, not a standard ISP ratio or a claim about a particular provider. Ask your supplier for its actual limits and blackholing policy.

Tbps-scale protection upstream

Cloudflare Magic Transit filters attack traffic across its global network before delivering legitimate traffic through the agreed path to your network. Its mitigation architecture is not defined as a simple multiple of your DIA circuit speed.

Your DIA bandwidth still governs the traffic your connection can carry. Large mitigation capacity does not turn a 1 Gbps circuit into a faster connection or guarantee every application remains available.

Why Axclusive uses Always-On

Protection in place before the attack starts.

Cloudflare Magic Transit supports Always-On and On-Demand deployments. Axclusive’s opt-in protection for DIA customers uses Always-On.

01

Always-On

Protected traffic continuously traverses Cloudflare. There is no need to first divert the protected prefixes into the mitigation network when an incident begins. Detection and filtering operate within that existing traffic path.

02

On-Demand

Traffic is diverted into Cloudflare when protection is activated. Detection, activation and routing convergence can introduce a period of exposure; the process depends on the monitoring and automation in place.

03

Our DIA offering

Opt in to Always-On protection for the agreed IPs on your Axclusive DIA service. We confirm addressing, routing, clean-traffic delivery and operational scope during onboarding. It is not automatically included with every DIA circuit.

Commercial terms differ between deployment modes. Compare a tailored quote against the cost of interrupted operations rather than assuming a fixed percentage price difference.

Questions before you plan

DDoS & Magic Transit FAQ

Clear answers for your service planning.

Does Axclusive offer Always-On DDoS protection with DIA?+
Yes. Axclusive offers Always-On Cloudflare Magic Transit protection to DIA customers who opt in. Protection covers the agreed IPs and traffic path after onboarding; it is not included automatically with every circuit. Contact us to assess IP transit or other connectivity requirements.
How is Tbps-scale mitigation different from my DIA speed?+
DIA speed describes the capacity of your internet connection. Mitigation capacity describes the scale of attack traffic the protection platform can absorb and filter. Cloudflare operates at a global Tbps scale; legitimate traffic delivered to you still needs to fit your circuit and network capacity.
Does every ISP blackhole attacks above ten times the circuit speed?+
No universal ratio applies. Mitigation limits and incident policies vary by service. Ask for the documented attack capacity and the conditions under which a provider may blackhole your IP. Blackholing a destination can drop legitimate traffic as well as attack traffic.
Why choose Always-On instead of On-Demand Magic Transit?+
With Always-On, protected traffic is already routed through Cloudflare, avoiding the initial diversion step required by an On-Demand deployment. On-Demand relies on detection and activation, with routing convergence to consider. Axclusive uses Always-On for DIA customers who opt in. Prices depend on the commercial scope, not a universal percentage difference.
Do I need my own /24?+
No. For this DIA offering, Axclusive supplies IP addresses from its own address space, so you do not need to own a /24 block. Always-On Cloudflare Magic Transit protection is available for customers who opt in, covering the agreed addresses. We confirm the allocation and routing during setup. Use of your own address space requires a separate eligibility and routing assessment.
Does Magic Transit replace a WAF or endpoint security?+
No. Network DDoS protection is one part of a wider security design. Application security, identity controls and endpoint protection need their own scope.
Will it guarantee no latency change or downtime?+
No universal promise is appropriate. Assess the delivery path and test the proposed configuration against agreed performance and availability requirements.
Is the price fixed regardless of configuration?+
Pricing and inclusions follow the proposal. Confirm protected prefixes, traffic measurement, delivery, service modes, add-ons and managed support.

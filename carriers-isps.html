Before you enquire

Questions from carriers & ISPs.

Can Axclusive support a wholesale customer project?+
Yes. Telcos, carriers and ISPs can discuss wholesale requirements directly with Axclusive, without joining the reseller and system integrator partner programme. Share the customer locations, service mix and delivery requirements; terms and responsibilities are agreed for the project.
Can we keep control of our BGP routing?+
Bring your ASN, prefixes and routing requirements to the IP transit discussion. The handoff, route feeds, policies and operational boundaries are confirmed in the design.
Can you connect sites outside a listed data centre?+
Axclusive offers Singapore-wide connectivity subject to address confirmation. Share both endpoints even if they are not in the reference directory; availability, access and delivery timing must be checked.
Does adding a second link guarantee resilience?+
No. Two links can share routes, equipment or building access. Agree the diversity, capacity and failover behaviour needed, then assess the complete proposed path.

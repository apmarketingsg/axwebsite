Before you enquire

Questions from businesses & enterprises.

Can an enterprise buy Data Center Interconnect?+
Yes. DCI describes connectivity between data centres, not a customer category. Businesses with infrastructure in multiple facilities can use it for private traffic, replication or recovery. Building-to-building and office-to-data-centre links can be discussed under leased lines.
Can you include broadband with Managed SD-WAN?+
Broadband can be included within the managed SD-WAN solution, subject to location and design. Axclusive does not present standalone shared business broadband as a current offering on this site.
Do enterprise customers need DDoS protection?+
Some do, especially where Internet-facing applications or connectivity are important to operations. Review the exposed services, address space and availability requirements; protection is an agreed service rather than an automatic inclusion.
Can you supply SIP trunks and manage our phone system?+
Yes. Axclusive supplies SIP trunks and offers managed Yeastar cloud PBX, with Microsoft Teams Calling and Webex Calling options to explore. Number porting is charged separately. Managed PBX support is 8×5 with a four-hour response target, with an optional 24×7 package; confirm the proposal scope.
Can we purchase equipment without a managed service?+
Yes. Equipment, subscriptions and renewals can be supplied separately. Axclusive implementation and operational support are limited to agreed managed-service and voice solutions.

Our story

From enterprise IT to the network behind it.

01

Founded in Singapore in 2013

Axclusive began by supporting enterprise IT infrastructure. Working with the systems inside a business showed us how much their performance depends on the connections beyond it.

02

Built and operated our own network

We developed our network under AS132337, bringing routing and connectivity into the same conversation as the infrastructure our customers run.

03

Expanded across Asia and Europe

Our core network presence spans Singapore, Hong Kong, Marseille and Amsterdam. Regional operations in Vietnam and India support our work across Asia-Pacific.

Useful answers

About Us FAQ

What does Axclusive do?+
Axclusive provides IP transit, Dedicated Internet Access, private connectivity, data centre and cloud connectivity, colocation, security, business voice and managed infrastructure services. Scope and availability are confirmed for each project.
Where are Axclusive’s core POPs?+
Axclusive’s four core POPs are Singapore, Hong Kong, Marseille and Amsterdam. Extended SD-WAN locations are assessed separately and are not additional Axclusive-owned POPs.
Does Axclusive serve enterprises as well as wholesale customers?+
Yes. Axclusive serves carriers, ISPs, hosting and cloud providers, and enterprises. The service design follows the customer’s applications, locations, routing and operating requirements.

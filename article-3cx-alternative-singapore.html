Axclusive Cloud PBX is a managed 3CX alternative for Singapore businesses that want the hosted phone system and SIP connection from one provider. The agreed Yeastar-powered package includes platform licensing, hosting, required voice interconnection, SIP trunk service and managed PBX operations. You do not need an on-site PBX server or a separate customer voice gateway for this integrated service.

Your current phone system may handle calls perfectly well. But when renewal approaches, the licence is only part of the decision. Who maintains the platform, changes the call flows and helps when a customer cannot get through? Review the complete service before deciding whether to renew or replace 3CX.

When should you consider a 3CX alternative?

A useful review starts with your own experience: the time spent administering the system, the features you actually use, the total renewal cost and the support responsibilities. Moving may make sense when you want to stop operating a PBX server, consolidate the SIP and phone-system providers, or agree a managed service around your team’s working hours.

If your current 3CX arrangement meets those needs, staying with it may be sensible. A change should solve an identified business problem, not simply replace one brand with another.

A brief perspective from Reddit

One self-hosting user running a small volunteering initiative described being happy with 3CX, but wrote:

“sadly the cost is getting too high to justify.”

Source: a contributor to r/3CX discussing licence fit and alternatives. Replies suggested other 3CX arrangements as well as competing products. This is an individual, unverified account—not representative customer research, current pricing evidence or an Axclusive testimonial.

Does 3CX require a Windows server?

No. 3CX documents Windows and Linux installation options alongside hosted deployments. Your existing system may already be hosted or managed by a partner. See the official 3CX administration manual.

If you operate your own PBX server or VM, Axclusive Cloud PBX removes that server from your own operational responsibilities. If your 3CX system is already managed, compare the included service, support and connectivity rather than assuming that moving to another cloud platform automatically reduces work or cost.

What is included in Axclusive Cloud PBX?

  • Yeastar PBX platform and licensing within the agreed package.
  • Cloud hosting and the required voice interconnection.
  • Axclusive SIP trunk service, with agreed numbers and call capacity.
  • Managed PBX operations within the agreed service scope.

Axclusive is the SIP trunk provider as well as the managed PBX provider, licensed by IMDA to provide IP telephony services. You can discuss the phone system and telephone connection with one team. Review Cloud PBX inclusions or explore our SIP integrations.

No separate customer voice gateway is needed for this integrated service. Supported phones and apps still require working Internet connectivity. The proposal defines users, concurrent calls, selected features and usage charges. Number porting is charged separately; handsets, headsets and specialist integrations are not automatically included.

Compare the full service before replacing 3CX

Compare equivalent users, call demand, essential features and support coverage over the same period. Axclusive Cloud PBX is not automatically cheaper for every deployment.

Plan a 3CX-to-Yeastar migration around your working day

Start with the calls you cannot afford to miss: the main reception number, sales enquiries, support queues and after-hours routing. Confirm how the new service will handle each one before scheduling the changeover.

  1. Document the current system. Record the 3CX version, users, numbers, menus, queues, opening hours, announcements, recordings and integrations.
  2. Check feature and device compatibility. Review exact phone models, firmware, apps and specialist endpoints. Confirm the proposed Yeastar edition supports the required functions.
  3. Agree the migration scope. Identify what can be transferred, what must be rebuilt and what needs separate retention. Do not assume every configuration, recording or integration imports automatically.
  4. Pilot the calling experience. Test inbound and outbound calls, transfers, caller ID, voicemail, queues and unanswered-call behaviour with representative users.
  5. Coordinate numbers and cutover. Confirm eligible number porting, charges, responsibilities, timing and a fallback plan. Keep the existing service active until the agreed transition is complete.

Existing extensions, advanced integrations and historical records need individual review. We do not promise a one-click migration or zero downtime before assessing the environment.

Who supports the replacement phone system?

Axclusive’s standard managed PBX support is 8×5 with a four-hour response target. Optional 24×7 coverage with a four-hour response target is available. A response target is not a guaranteed resolution time.

The agreement defines support hours, escalation, user and call-flow changes, updates, monitoring, backups and other managed tasks. See the managed Cloud PBX service for the support options.

Is Axclusive Cloud PBX the right replacement?

It is worth evaluating if you want a managed phone system and SIP service from one provider. Confirm the feature fit and total cost before making a decision. If your priority is calling within an existing collaboration platform, also compare Microsoft Teams Calling and Webex Calling through Axclusive.

Share your 3CX version, renewal date, user count, concurrent calls, business numbers and essential features. Ask Axclusive to review your Cloud PBX migration requirements before you commit to a replacement.

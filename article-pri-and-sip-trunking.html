Comparing PRI and SIP Trunking for Enterprise Communication

May 28, 2026 · Axclusive · Updated 17 November 2026

PRI and SIP trunking are two ways of connecting a business phone system to external telephone calls. For Singapore deployments, the relevant PRI comparison is normally E1 with 30 voice channels—not the 23-channel T1 format used in other markets.

PRI vs SIP Trunking Key Differences

A full E1 PRI provides 30 bearer channels plus a signalling channel. T1 PRI provides 23 bearer channels plus signalling. SIP trunking carries calls over an IP connection with a contracted number of simultaneous sessions; it is scalable within service and system limits, not unlimited.

PRI vs SIP trunking advantages and limitations

PRI avoids packet queuing on the access circuit, but that does not guarantee perfect audio or immunity from outages. Circuit faults, PBX failures and power loss still matter. A physical circuit is not an encryption mechanism.

SIP allows flexible IP-based delivery and can suit hosted or on-site systems. Audio quality depends on the complete media path. QoS on controlled networks, adequate bandwidth and tested recovery can help; they cannot guarantee uninterrupted calls. Neither option is automatically cheaper for every business.

PRI vs SIP trunking migration considerations

  • Inventory the existing PBX, numbers, call volumes and E1 interfaces. Confirm whether a gateway, platform upgrade or hosted replacement is appropriate.
  • Compare quotes using the same users, concurrent calls, destinations, licences and support period. Read the actual SLA rather than assuming a universal availability figure.
  • Test incoming/outgoing calls, caller ID, transfers, emergency-call arrangements and any fax or specialist devices.
  • Agree number-porting eligibility, charges, timing and fallback. Keep the existing service until the agreed cutover is complete.

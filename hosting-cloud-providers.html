Before you enquire

Questions from hosting & cloud providers.

Can you combine colocation, IP transit and private links?+
Discuss them as one infrastructure requirement. Facility availability, rack and power needs, transit handoff, private endpoints and operating responsibilities are confirmed in the proposal.
Do we need our own ASN for every service?+
No single requirement applies to all services. Bring your ASN and prefix details for an IP transit discussion; DIA, private connectivity and other options have their own addressing and routing requirements.
Can you steer traffic between our CDN providers?+
Axclusive’s Managed CDN offering can use smart DNS to steer between contracted CDN providers according to agreed real-user performance, health and traffic-split policies. DNS caching and service configuration affect when changes take effect.
Is DDoS protection automatically included with hosting connectivity?+
Do not assume it is included. Discuss the protected services, IP address space, connectivity and commercial scope. Axclusive offers Always-On Cloudflare Magic Transit protection to DIA customers who opt in, with deployment details confirmed for the proposed service.

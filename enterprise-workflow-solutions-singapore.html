Useful answers

Questions before you choose

How do we start a ServiceNow workflow discussion?+
Choose one request process and identify its users, owner, approvals, systems and reporting needs. Assess IT service management, portfolio or employee-service workflows against that requirement. Agree licensing, configuration, integrations, data responsibilities and acceptance criteria in the proposal; do not assume all delivery work is included in a subscription.
Which ServiceNow areas can we discuss?+
IT Service Management, Strategic Portfolio Management (formerly IT Business Management) and HR Service Delivery. Confirm the selected applications, licences and delivery responsibilities in the proposal.
Is this the same as managed infrastructure?+
No. Workflow applications organise how work is requested, approved and tracked. Managed infrastructure covers agreed operational tasks for systems and networks; the two can complement each other.
Is every integration or implementation included?+
No. Existing systems, licence entitlements, project work and ongoing support are assessed and scoped explicitly. There is no blanket implementation inclusion with product supply.

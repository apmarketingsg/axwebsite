Choose the network behind your SD-WAN

Intelligent internet connectivity or an international backbone?

Both approaches use Fortinet SD-WAN policies. The difference is the transport carrying your traffic between locations.

1. Internet-based SD-WAN

Intelligent VPN, traffic steering and failover. Extend site-to-site connectivity with encrypted tunnels where required, automated link-quality checks and application-aware path selection.

Combine two internet circuits from different ISPs, or fixed broadband with 4G/5G backup. A hybrid design can also combine MPLS with internet access; that arrangement is not purely internet-based.

Often a good fit for local branches and in-country applications where the available access paths meet performance needs. It can also support international traffic, although public internet routing remains outside the managed backbone design.

Focus: resilience, application policies and cost efficiency. Savings depend on the circuits, equipment and management scope.

2. Backbone-enabled SD-WAN

SD-WAN flexibility with managed international transport. Route selected inter-site traffic through Axclusive’s regional and international backbone instead of relying entirely on public internet paths between sites.

Consider this approach for overseas offices, cross-border business applications and latency-sensitive workloads. A managed backbone can offer more predictable routing and opportunities to improve latency and packet loss, depending on the endpoints and access paths.

Evaluate it as an alternative to, or a phased migration from, traditional MPLS. Review routing, security, application performance and service commitments before retiring existing links.

Focus: control over the inter-site path, international reach and route-specific service commitments.

Resilience is a design choice

Two links do not automatically mean two independent paths.

SD-WAN can select paths using configured policies and supported health measurements. It does not create extra physical capacity, remove congestion from every destination or guarantee uninterrupted application sessions.

Confirm shared risks, failover behaviour and application recovery during testing. A single session should not be assumed to combine the full speed of both links.

Questions before you plan

Managed SD-WAN FAQ

Clear answers for your service planning.

Is backbone-enabled SD-WAN limited to Axclusive’s four POPs?+
No. We extend our international backbone-enabled SD-WAN offering through partner infrastructure with a compute footprint spanning 58 locations worldwide. Virtual gateways can be deployed where suitable virtual machines and network connectivity are available. The count includes cities that overlap with our core POPs; each deployment is subject to capacity, compatibility, licensing, routing and access confirmation.
What is the difference between internet-based and backbone-enabled SD-WAN?+
Internet-based SD-WAN uses available internet connections for intelligent path selection and site-to-site tunnels. Backbone-enabled SD-WAN adds managed provider transport for selected inter-site routes. Axclusive offers both and can combine them within one network.
Does a backbone guarantee lower latency than the internet?+
No universal comparison applies. A managed backbone offers more control over the selected route, but performance also depends on endpoints, last-mile access, congestion and design. We assess your applications and agree measurable commitments for the proposed service.
Can SD-WAN replace our MPLS network?+
It can be an alternative for suitable workloads or coexist with MPLS during migration. Axclusive assesses the traffic, security, latency, resilience and service requirements before proposing a replacement. Existing MPLS can also be one of the underlying connections.
Can I order this SD-WAN service now?+
Yes. Axclusive has the infrastructure and capabilities to deliver managed Fortinet SD-WAN. We assess your sites and applications, then confirm the access links, equipment, service scope and installation schedule.
Will it be bundled with Axclusive DIA or broadband?+
Yes. We can bundle managed SD-WAN with Axclusive DIA or broadband supplied as part of the managed solution. Existing circuits, MPLS and cellular backup can be assessed as part of the overall design.
Does SD-WAN replace a leased line?+
Not in every design. SD-WAN manages traffic across underlying links. A private leased line and Internet access have different connectivity characteristics; choose based on the application and security requirements.
Does dual connectivity guarantee no downtime?+
No. Links can share infrastructure, and failover can affect sessions. Physical diversity, equipment, power and application recovery need to be designed and tested.
What are the prices and support hours?+
Pricing and support are scoped to your deployment. The proposal identifies hardware, subscriptions, access circuits, any backbone transport, implementation, support hours and response targets.

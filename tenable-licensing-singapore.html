Useful answers

Questions before you choose

Does a Tenable licence include a managed security service?+
A licence or renewal supplies the agreed product entitlement. It does not by itself include a managed SOC, vulnerability assessment project or remediation service. Confirm the product, licensed scope, term and vendor support before purchase, and separately identify who will operate it.
Can we buy Tenable products without a managed service?+
Yes. Product supply and applicable renewals can be purchased separately. Axclusive implementation and ongoing support are not included with a product-only order.
What information is needed for a quotation?+
Share product or edition, quantities or asset counts, subscription term, current renewal details and the required delivery date.
Does vendor support mean Axclusive manages the product?+
No. Vendor support entitlements and Axclusive managed-service responsibilities are separate and must be stated in the quotation or agreement.

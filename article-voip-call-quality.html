VoIP Call Quality Tips to Improve Business Calls

March 26, 2026 · Axclusive · Updated 17 November 2026

In this article

Browse all blog ↗

Good VoIP call quality depends on the full path: phones, headsets, office networking, access links and the voice platform. Diagnose the cause before buying more bandwidth.

VoIP Call Quality Influencing Factors

  • Delay: use less than 150 ms one-way as a common voice-design target, not a round-trip measurement or a hard universal limit. Long geographic routes may exceed it.
  • Loss: target below 1%, preferably as close to zero as practical. Burst loss can be disruptive even when the average looks low; 5% is not a suitable general quality target.
  • Jitter: below 30 ms is a common design target. Jitter buffers trade smoother playback against additional delay.
  • Endpoints: overloaded devices, poor headsets, acoustic echo and Wi-Fi interference can harm calls even with adequate Internet bandwidth.

These are planning targets from voice-design guidance, not a promise that every call below them will sound perfect. Codec, packet timing, burst behaviour and user expectations also matter.

Ways to Improve VoIP Call Quality

  • Use wired connections where practical and test Wi-Fi coverage where it is needed.
  • Apply QoS at bottlenecks your organisation controls. It helps prioritise voice there; it cannot guarantee priority across unrelated Internet networks.
  • Allow only the required signalling and media flows according to the service design. Do not disable the firewall indiscriminately.
  • Check firmware, headset quality and acoustic conditions. Wider-band codecs can improve clarity when both endpoints and the call path support them.

Troubleshooting VoIP Call Quality Issues

Record the time, caller/callee, affected sites and whether the issue occurs in one or both directions. Compare call statistics during good and bad periods. Check packet loss, delay, jitter, interface utilisation and device health before changing settings.

Test backup links and rerouting deliberately. A successful new call after failover does not prove that an existing call survives. Agree escalation and provide the voice provider with call examples and measurements.

VoIP Call Quality FAQ

Does more bandwidth always fix poor calls?

No. It helps when capacity is the bottleneck, but it does not fix bad Wi-Fi, endpoint faults, routing delay or incorrect media/NAT settings.

What does a quality test show?

It measures conditions on the tested path and time. Estimated MOS scores can help compare performance, but a single speed test or score does not establish service reliability.

Where should a small office start?

Check the headset and wired connection, measure busy-hour usage, verify the service configuration and review bandwidth per simultaneous call.

CWDM vs DWDM Which Optical Network Solution Is Better

August 19, 2026 · Axclusive · Updated 17 November 2026

Browse all blog ↗

CWDM and DWDM increase fibre capacity by carrying separate optical channels on the same infrastructure. Choose between them using the required capacity, route loss, growth plan and equipment compatibility. Neither name alone establishes a guaranteed distance, total throughput, price or level of resilience.

CWDM versus DWDM: the practical differences

The ITU CWDM grid uses 20 nm wavelength spacing, with nominal wavelengths from 1271 to 1611 nm. This offers up to 18 grid positions, but deployed systems may use fewer because of the chosen optics, mux and fibre characteristics. DWDM uses closer frequency spacing and can use fixed or flexible grids. Common planning widths include 50, 75 and 100 GHz, depending on the implementation.

Channel spacing is not the same as modulation or data rate. CWDM is often attractive for relatively simple links; DWDM provides more options for high-capacity amplified systems. A passive mux consumes no power in either family, while transceivers and active transport equipment do. Management and protection come from the overall system design—not from the CWDM/DWDM label.

Do not assign CWDM a universal 160 km maximum or DWDM an unlimited reach. Distance depends on transmit power, receiver sensitivity, fibre attenuation, dispersion, connectors and passive losses. Standard C-band EDFAs do not amplify the entire CWDM wavelength range; this limits simple amplification choices, but does not mean all CWDM-related amplification is impossible.

Likewise, there is no universal 100 Gbps-per-channel ceiling or 400 Gbps-total limit defined just by the CWDM grid. Throughput depends on the equipment and channels actually deployed. For DWDM, a large channel count on a 50 GHz system cannot simply be multiplied by 400G: conventional 400ZR/ZR+ channels need at least 75 GHz spacing and suitable passbands. Read the 400G spacing and capacity explanation.

Compare the complete design

There is no reliable universal rule that DWDM equipment costs four or five times more, or that its transceivers are a fixed percentage cheaper. Compare like-for-like reach, capacity, protection, support and power requirements. Nor should a financial application be promised instantaneous replication: fibre propagation and application behaviour impose real delay.

Questions before choosing

Can CWDM and DWDM coexist? Some designs place DWDM channels within a suitable CWDM passband. This needs spectral compatibility and a validated loss budget; it is not a universal plug-in upgrade.

Do we need to manage the optical network ourselves? Axclusive’s MOFN service combines dark fibre, DWDM equipment and managed service, with protected or unprotected options. If you only need a connection between agreed sites, compare leased lines and DCI before choosing your own optical platform.

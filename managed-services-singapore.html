What should a managed service do?

Keep the whole service running, from connectivity to the application.

A working connection is only one dependency. A server running short of resources, an operating system awaiting patches or a database issue can still interrupt the service your users need. Managed infrastructure and IT services bring these layers into a shared operating plan.

Axclusive combines infrastructure management with operation of its own network. Choose the systems and recurring responsibilities you want us to own, while retaining business decisions and application ownership within your team. Our Cisco Networking Partner and Managed Services Practice certifications relate to Cisco; support for other platforms is confirmed in your proposal.

One operating plan across your IT environment

Choose the management your systems need.

Build the service around named assets, supported platforms and agreed responsibilities. These capabilities can be combined; the proposal defines what is included.

01

Server infrastructure management

Manage the agreed physical or virtual server estate, including monitoring, configuration, resource utilisation and capacity planning. Identify infrastructure dependencies before they affect business services.

02

Operating system management

Manage supported operating systems, routine administration, updates and operational issues. Agree supported versions, access privileges and maintenance responsibilities for each system.

03

Application & database management

Manage agreed applications and databases, with service-health monitoring, operational troubleshooting and maintenance. Define supported products, database responsibilities and vendor escalation; bespoke development is a separate requirement to assess.

04

Patching & change management

Plan patch cycles across the systems in scope. Agree compatibility checks, approvals, maintenance windows, rollback arrangements and post-change validation, including how urgent updates are handled.

05

Network & connectivity management

Manage agreed routers, switches, firewalls, Wi-Fi and WAN infrastructure. Include Managed SD-WAN where required and coordinate network issues with the server and application layers.

06

Customer success & service governance

Keep the service aligned with your priorities through an agreed customer-success contact, service reviews, reporting and improvement planning. Review recurring issues, risks, capacity and upcoming business changes together.

Managed networks across your sites

Managed SD-WAN belongs in your operating plan.

Connect offices, data centres and applications with Fortinet-based Managed SD-WAN. Axclusive combines the SD-WAN solution with connectivity and an agreed operational support scope.

01

Internet-based SD-WAN

Use intelligent path selection, link monitoring and failover across suitable Internet, MPLS or mobile connections. Plan around application needs, site access and the links available at each location.

02

Backbone-enabled SD-WAN

For cross-border sites and workloads, combine Fortinet SD-WAN with Axclusive’s international backbone and supported extended locations. Confirm routing, availability and performance commitments for the proposed paths.

03

Connectivity and operations together

Bundle DIA or broadband within the managed solution. Agree device management, monitoring, traffic policies, changes, incident escalation and support responsibilities as part of the service.

Service levels that mean something

Separate coverage, response and resolution.

Support hours tell you when the service operates. A response target sets when a request is acknowledged or acted upon under the agreement. Resolution depends on the incident and the agreed obligations.

Confirm severity definitions, escalation, maintenance windows, reporting and exclusions. Managed PBX support packages do not automatically define the terms for every managed IT service.

Make the SLA and responsibilities explicit

Document the asset and platform list, recurring activities, severity levels, coverage hours, response targets, escalation contacts, maintenance windows and reporting cadence. State any restoration or availability commitments, measurement methods, dependencies and exclusions. Confirm backup, recovery and handover responsibilities separately.

Questions before you plan

Managed Services FAQ

Clear answers for your service planning.

Do you manage servers, operating systems, applications and databases?+
Yes. Axclusive offers management across these layers as well as networks. We confirm the named systems, supported versions, activities, permissions and vendor dependencies before agreeing the service.
Can patching be included in the managed service?+
Yes. Patching can form part of the agreed scope. We define the systems covered, patch cadence, approvals, maintenance windows, validation and rollback arrangements. Major upgrades and application changes are assessed separately.
What does customer success include?+
An agreed customer-success contact and review process help align service delivery with your priorities. The proposal defines review frequency, reporting, escalation and improvement planning.
Do you provide Managed SD-WAN as part of managed services?+
Yes. Axclusive offers Fortinet-based Managed SD-WAN using Internet-based or international backbone-enabled connectivity. DIA or broadband can be included within the managed solution. We agree device management, monitoring, traffic policies, changes and support for your sites. Explore Managed SD-WAN.
Can you manage only part of our environment?+
Yes. Discuss a defined scope alongside your internal team or other providers. The proposal identifies assets, responsibilities and exclusions.
Are monitoring and backups always included?+
No universal inclusion is assumed. Monitoring, backup jobs, restoration, updates and reporting must be stated for the systems covered.
Does your Cisco certification apply to every vendor?+
Our stated Cisco credentials relate to that programme. Other platforms and technologies are assessed and scoped separately; no additional vendor certification is implied.
What support hours are available?+
Coverage and response commitments are agreed for the particular service. The published managed PBX packages are specific to that offering, not a blanket commitment for all IT support.
Can connectivity be included?+
Discuss Axclusive connectivity alongside equipment and operations. The proposal separates circuit commitments from the managed service responsibilities.

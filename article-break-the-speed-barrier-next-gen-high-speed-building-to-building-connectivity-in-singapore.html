In today’s digital age, speed is everything. Whether you're running a tech-driven enterprise, media production house, or a data-heavy operation, the ability to transfer large volumes of data quickly and securely is critical. Yet many businesses in Singapore are still stuck with outdated MPLS solutions that simply can’t keep up with modern bandwidth demands.

If you’re looking to break free from traditional bottlenecks and embrace next-generation connectivity, this is the right time to upgrade.

The Limitations of Traditional MPLS in Singapore

Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS) has long been the go-to for enterprise connectivity. However, it comes with limitations:

  • Lower bandwidth limits (typically capped at 1G or below)
  • High costs for additional capacity
  • Long provisioning times
  • Lack of flexibility and scalability

For modern businesses transferring large media files, backups, or real-time analytics, traditional MPLS simply isn’t enough.

Why Choose 10G or 100G Building-to-Building Connectivity?

 
With high-speed point-to-point connectivity options now available in Singapore, companies can achieve:

  • Ultra-fast data transfers between buildings, campuses, or data centers
  • Low-latency performance critical for applications like video editing, cloud workloads, or financial trading
  • Enhanced scalability for future growth
  • Improved cost-efficiency with better value per Gbps

Whether you’re an IT service provider, content creator, cloud-based startup, or enterprise looking to future-proof your network, high-bandwidth connectivity is no longer a luxury — it’s a necessity.

Use Cases Across Industries
  • Media & Broadcasting: Transfer uncompressed 4K/8K footage between production offices and data centers without delay.
  • Finance & Trading: Gain an edge with low-latency, high-throughput data feeds.
  • IT & Cloud Services: Move large datasets, backups, or VM migrations effortlessly across sites.
  • Healthcare & Research: Share high-resolution imaging data or genomic datasets securely and quickly.

Break the Speed Barrier: Next-Gen High-Speed Building-to-Building Connectivity in Singapore

May 28, 2025 · Axclusive · Updated 17 November 2026

A slow transfer between an office and a data centre can delay backups, media production and access to shared systems. A private building-to-building connection gives agreed sites dedicated service capacity, but the right speed depends on the workload and the complete path.

MPLS is not inherently limited to 1 Gbps

MPLS is a forwarding technology, not a fixed bandwidth ceiling. Available access speeds and commercial terms depend on the provider and product. Compare the offered capacity, SLA, route and price rather than assuming every MPLS service is slow or obsolete.

Choose capacity for the transfer window

Axclusive offers private connectivity options from 1 Gbps to 100 Gbps, subject to location and service design. For 10G or 100G requirements, confirm sustained throughput across servers, storage, firewalls and interfaces. Line rate alone does not guarantee application throughput.

Use cases for hosting and enterprise teams

  • Transfer large media assets between production facilities and storage.
  • Replicate backups or databases to another site within the required recovery window.
  • Connect hosting platforms across data centres.
  • Link an office or campus to privately hosted applications.

Choose the right private service

Leased Lines connect agreed buildings or other private endpoints. Data Center Interconnect focuses on links between data centres. Managed Optical Fiber Network combines the agreed dark-fibre route, DWDM equipment and managed service. Confirm whether protected or unprotected connectivity is appropriate.

Plan resilience and security

Private connectivity is not automatically encrypted. Discuss encryption, route diversity, failover behaviour and SLA boundaries. Provisioning dates depend on address checks, building access, cross-connects and capacity. Request an address and capacity assessment.

Product supply & renewal questions

Which Cisco products can I enquire about?+
Discuss Catalyst switches, Nexus switches, routing, security, Duo and collaboration products. Exact models, licences and availability are confirmed in your quotation.
Does a Cisco equipment purchase include configuration or installation?+
No. Standalone product supply does not include Axclusive configuration, installation or network consulting. Implementation and support are limited to agreed managed-service and voice solutions.
What Cisco credentials does Axclusive hold?+
Axclusive is a certified Cisco Networking Partner and holds certification for its Managed Services Practice. These credentials do not change the scope of a product-only purchase.
Can you quote Cisco licences or vendor support renewals?+
Send the current product details, licence quantities and expiry dates. Applicable renewal options and vendor support entitlements are confirmed before ordering.
Where do I enquire about a managed Cisco network or voice solution?+
Use our Managed IT & Networks or Webex Calling pages to discuss a managed-service or voice requirement. Implementation and ongoing support are defined within that agreed service scope.

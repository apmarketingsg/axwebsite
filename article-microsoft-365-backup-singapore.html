Microsoft 365 Backup in Singapore: What Should Your Business Protect?

November 16, 2026 · Axclusive

Microsoft 365 backup planning starts with the data you need to recover, how far back you need to go and who will perform the restore. Platform availability, retention settings and backup recovery address different needs. Review the controls you already have before buying an additional service.

A colleague deletes a shared folder. A departing employee’s mailbox contains important correspondence. An administrator needs to recover a larger set of changed files. These situations can require different recovery points, permissions and tools.

Check existing Microsoft 365 recovery controls first

Microsoft provides recovery and retention capabilities, and also offers a Microsoft 365 Backup product. Its documented workload scope includes Exchange mailboxes, OneDrive accounts and SharePoint sites. Do not assume that a standard Microsoft 365 subscription includes every backup feature, or that Microsoft provides no backup options.

Review Microsoft’s current Backup overview alongside your licences and configuration. Retention policies serve retention and deletion requirements; compare their recovery behaviour with the specific incident you need to handle.

Create a workload and restore checklist

  • Email: user and shared mailboxes, required historical data and item-level restore needs.
  • Files: OneDrive and SharePoint content, permissions and recovery destination.
  • Teams: identify files, conversations and configuration separately; confirm exact product coverage rather than assuming the entire collaboration workspace can be restored.
  • Departing users: agree retention, licence implications and access after offboarding.

Druva publishes a Microsoft 365 coverage matrix. Use current platform documentation and a scoped test to confirm the features you require.

Ask these questions before ordering

  1. What is protected, and how are newly added users or sites enrolled?
  2. How long are recovery points retained, and how frequently are backups completed?
  3. Where is backup data stored, and does that location meet your requirements?
  4. Who can change policies, delete recovery points and request a restore?
  5. Who investigates failed jobs, and what support coverage applies?
  6. What storage, onboarding, restore or transfer charges apply?

Set measurable RTO and RPO targets for important workloads. Test a restore before relying on the arrangement. Singapore customer support does not automatically mean Singapore data residency.

Discuss a managed backup service with Axclusive

Axclusive’s backup and recovery service includes a Druva option with AWS-hosted storage within the agreed package. NAKIVO with Axclusive-managed S3-compatible storage is another backup model; suitability for your Microsoft 365 workload and required features must be confirmed before ordering.

Bring your user count, workloads, retention requirements and existing licences. We will define the platform, storage and managed responsibilities in the proposal. Buying Microsoft 365 licences alone does not include a separately managed backup service.

If you need protection against deletion of recovery points, also review immutable backups and their limits.

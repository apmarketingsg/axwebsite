Cloudflare services: match protection to the workload

A company protecting an IP network has different requirements from a team protecting a website or API. Cloudflare offers several product families; purchasing one does not automatically include the others. Axclusive can discuss product supply and agreed managed connectivity or security scope, with responsibilities stated in the proposal.

Start with what must remain available

Identify the public networks, applications and customer journeys affected by an outage. Record normal traffic, important user markets, current security controls and recovery priorities. Avoid selecting solely on a headline network-capacity figure: global capacity changes over time and is not a customer-specific service guarantee.

Separate network and application protection

These are product-selection categories, not a promise that every item is included in every Axclusive package. Implementation and ongoing Axclusive support apply only within an agreed managed-service or voice solution. Product subscriptions and vendor support entitlements are distinct from Axclusive operational management.

For our opt-in DIA protection, Axclusive offers Always-On Magic Transit. Axclusive-assigned service IP addresses remain Axclusive-owned. Discuss prefix eligibility, traffic delivery, support and SLA before ordering.

Define results that can be measured

Agree success criteria before deployment: permitted application traffic, observed latency, attack visibility, escalation ownership and recovery behaviour. Test those criteria and review them over time. No protection product makes a business immune to every attack. This page describes an approach, not a verified customer case study or a promise of uninterrupted operation.

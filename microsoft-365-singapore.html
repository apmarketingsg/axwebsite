Before you order

Product supply & renewal questions

Can I renew an existing Office 365 subscription?+
Send the exact Microsoft 365 or Office 365 plan name, quantities and expiry date. We will confirm the applicable renewal options, eligibility and terms.
Does licence supply include setup, migration or user support?+
No. Standalone subscription supply does not include tenant setup, email migration, administration or Axclusive technical support. Implementation and support are limited to agreed managed-service and voice solutions.
Does every Microsoft 365 plan include Teams?+
No. Plans are available with and without Teams. Confirm the selected plan and its entitlements. Teams Phone and PSTN calling requirements are considered separately.
What should I send for a licence quotation?+
Send the subscription names, required licence quantities, term and start or renewal date. Do not send account passwords or administrator credentials.

Product supply & renewal questions

Can we buy products without a managed service?+
Yes. Product supply and renewals can be purchased on their own. They do not include Axclusive implementation or ongoing technical support.
Is this an online shop with live stock and pricing?+
No. This is a quotation-based catalogue of product categories. Send the exact items and quantities so we can confirm availability, pricing, entitlements and delivery timing.
Do you provide standalone installation or consultancy for these products?+
Axclusive implementation and ongoing support are available only as part of agreed managed-service or business voice solutions. Standalone product purchases and renewals do not include these services.
Are vendor warranty and support included?+
Only the warranty and support entitlements stated in your quotation apply. Vendor entitlements are separate from Axclusive managed support.
Can you supply other brands?+
Send the brand, product details and quantities. We will confirm whether the requested items can be supplied; the listed categories are not a guarantee of stock or availability.

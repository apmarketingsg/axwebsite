Practical answers

Before you choose

Which IT products does Axclusive resell?+
Axclusive resells products from Cisco, Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks and Microsoft, including Microsoft 365. Send your product requirements for confirmation of the available models, editions and supply terms. Other brands can be discussed as part of an enquiry.
Can we buy products without a managed service?+
Yes. Product supply, implementation and ongoing management are separate scopes. Your quotation identifies what you are buying and which services are included.
Can you help with an existing licence renewal?+
Send the current product or subscription, quantities and renewal date. We will review the required renewal scope and confirm eligibility, term and pricing before you place an order.
Can equipment and connectivity be planned together?+
Yes. Discuss your equipment alongside Dedicated Internet Access, IP transit, private connectivity or Managed SD-WAN. The design and service availability are confirmed for your locations.

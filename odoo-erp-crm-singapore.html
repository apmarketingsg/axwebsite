Start with the workflow

When growth creates more handovers.

A sales order should not need to be retyped into several spreadsheets before it reaches finance or operations. When teams work from different records, customers wait and managers lose visibility.

Odoo brings business applications such as CRM, sales and inventory into a connected platform. CRM focuses on prospects and customer relationships; ERP connects wider operational processes and records. The useful starting point is the handover between teams: what data needs to move, who approves it and what the next person needs to see.

Odoo ERP & CRM use cases

Connect the work between your teams.

Sales and customer relationships

When an enquiry becomes an order, the next team needs the customer details, agreed scope and status. Plan lead capture, opportunity tracking and quotation handovers around the information sales and operations need to share.

Orders, inventory and purchasing

When an order changes, purchasing and fulfilment need the updated requirements. Map stock checks, purchasing approvals and delivery records before choosing modules. Agree how exceptions and existing systems will be handled.

Finance and business visibility

Give finance and managers a clearer view of agreed operational records. Define invoicing handovers, reporting and reconciliation needs with your finance team. Confirm Singapore accounting requirements and any required integrations for the selected edition and configuration.

Odoo ERP & CRM FAQ

Should we configure Odoo or build custom software?+
Start by assessing whether standard Odoo workflows meet the requirement. Custom development may be appropriate for a specific gap, integration or distinct business process. Compare the delivery effort, upgrade implications and ongoing ownership before deciding. Axclusive scopes application development separately from software subscriptions.
How much does an Odoo project in Singapore cost?+
Separate software subscriptions from configuration, data migration, integrations, custom development, training and ongoing management. Cost depends on the edition, users, workflow complexity and delivery scope. Ask for clear deliverables, assumptions and acceptance criteria; licensing alone does not include a completed rollout.
What should we prepare for an Odoo project?+
Bring one end-to-end workflow, the teams involved, existing systems, sample record types and the reports you need. Identify approval steps, data owners and the problems you want to remove. Axclusive can then assess modules, migration and integrations against an agreed initial scope.
Should we implement every Odoo module at once?+
Start with a defined business process and dependencies. A phased scope can make data preparation, testing and user adoption easier to manage.
Can existing business systems be integrated?+
We assess the systems, available interfaces, data flows and access requirements before agreeing integration work. Compatibility and delivery scope must be confirmed.
Are implementation and ongoing support included in licensing?+
No. Licensing, project delivery and ongoing managed support are separate scopes. Your proposal identifies the responsibilities and deliverables for each.

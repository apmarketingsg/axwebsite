Start with the workflow

When growth creates more handovers.

A sales order should not need to be retyped into several spreadsheets before it reaches finance or operations. When teams work from different records, customers wait and managers lose visibility.

Axclusive helps businesses assess Odoo ERP and CRM requirements around their actual workflows. Start with the process that creates the most repeated work, then agree the applications, data and integrations needed to improve it.

Odoo ERP & CRM use cases

Connect the work between your teams.

Sales and customer relationships

Plan lead capture, opportunity tracking, quotation and sales handovers so the next team has the information it needs.

Orders, inventory and purchasing

Assess how orders move through purchasing, stock and delivery. Agree the workflows and records your operation needs before choosing modules.

Finance and business visibility

Bring finance requirements and operational reporting into the discussion. Confirm local accounting needs, reporting responsibilities and any required integrations.

A practical rollout

Build around the business, then the modules.

Map one complete process

Identify users, approvals, current data and the points where work is delayed. Agree measurable acceptance criteria for the initial phase.

Define the delivery scope

Specify editions, licences, configuration, data migration, integrations and custom development. Each is scoped separately; an application subscription alone does not include a project.

Plan adoption and ownership

Agree testing, training, handover and post-launch responsibilities. Ongoing application and database management can be considered under a separate managed-service scope.

Useful answers

Questions before you choose

Should we implement every Odoo module at once?+
Start with a defined business process and dependencies. A phased scope can make data preparation, testing and user adoption easier to manage.
Can existing business systems be integrated?+
We assess the systems, available interfaces, data flows and access requirements before agreeing integration work. Compatibility and delivery scope must be confirmed.
Are implementation and ongoing support included in licensing?+
No. Licensing, project delivery and ongoing managed support are separate scopes. Your proposal identifies the responsibilities and deliverables for each.

What is colocation?

Keep control of your hardware.
Plan beyond the server room.

Colocation houses your servers and network equipment in a data centre, with the space, power, cooling and connectivity your deployment requires. You keep your equipment while choosing the operating support that fits your team.

For a hosting provider, the next constraint may be customer growth. For an enterprise, it may be an office move, a system refresh or a recovery project. Start with what the infrastructure needs to do, then select the facility and network around it.

Colocation pricing

Understand the power.
Compare the whole proposal.

Axclusive uses kW-based power pricing. Compare the committed or metered basis, included rack space and connectivity as well as the headline rate.

Ask for a quotation based on your hardware, power draw and growth needs. Confirm setup fees, cross-connects, remote hands, taxes and any minimum commitment before comparing options.

kW, kVA and kWh mean different things

kW measures real power. kVA measures apparent power. Their relationship is kW = kVA × power factor.

For example, 1 kVA at a power factor of 0.8 corresponds to 0.8 kW. kWh measures energy consumed over time. Ask how each quote defines and bills the allowance; a kW rate alone does not establish a usage-only billing model.

Questions before you move

Colocation FAQ

Space, power, connectivity and operating scope.

What is colocation?+
Colocation places your own servers and network equipment in a data centre, with space, power, cooling and connectivity arranged for the deployment. You retain your hardware; operating-system and application management are included only within an agreed managed-service scope.
Can hosting providers and enterprises use Axclusive colocation?+
Yes. Hosting providers can plan customer platforms and network infrastructure; enterprises can plan business systems, hybrid cloud and recovery infrastructure. The facility, power, space and operating scope are confirmed for each deployment.
Can I start with a smaller deployment?+
Share the number of servers, rack units, power draw and growth plan. Axclusive assesses smaller requirements as well as expansion projects. Available space, minimum commitments and supported power density are confirmed in your quotation.
Which connectivity services can I combine with colocation?+
Depending on the location and design, discuss IP transit, Dedicated Internet Access, private leased lines, Data Center Interconnect and cloud or multi-service connectivity. Cross-connects, handoffs, capacity and route diversity are confirmed for the selected facility.
Is colocation available at every location in your coverage directory?+
No. The directory lists connectivity locations, not guaranteed colocation inventory. Available rack space, power, access and connectivity need separate confirmation for the chosen facility.
How is colocation priced?+
Axclusive uses a kW-based power pricing approach. Your quotation should confirm the power commitment or metering basis, space, connectivity, cross-connects, setup, remote hands, taxes and support inclusions. kW describes real power; kWh describes energy used over time.
Can Axclusive help with Hong Kong colocation?+
Yes. Discuss Singapore and Hong Kong requirements with Axclusive. Capacity, facility details, commercial terms and inter-site connectivity are confirmed for your project.
Does colocation automatically include disaster recovery or managed servers?+
No. Recovery needs a design covering replication, backup, failure domains and testing. Hardware support, remote hands, monitoring and managed operating systems or applications each need an agreed scope.

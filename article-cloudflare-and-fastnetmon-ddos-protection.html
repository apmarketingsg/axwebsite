Cloudflare and FastNetMon DDoS Protection for High Performance Network Security

DDoS detection and attack mitigation perform different jobs. FastNetMon analyses traffic telemetry and can trigger configured responses; an upstream scrubbing service can absorb and filter attack traffic before it saturates a smaller access circuit. Neither should be described as a guarantee that legitimate traffic is never affected.

Detection, filtering and upstream capacity

A local filter cannot restore capacity on an upstream link that is already full of attack traffic. A useful design combines appropriate upstream protection with visibility and a response policy. ISP capabilities vary, so compare the actual solution rather than assuming every competitor requires manual tickets or can only blackhole traffic.

What FastNetMon can contribute

FastNetMon can consume NetFlow, IPFIX, sFlow or other supported telemetry. Visibility depends on the configured source, export intervals, sampling and collector capacity. Flow records are not a complete packet capture, and sampled data is not a record of every packet. Detection time varies with those settings and thresholds; do not assume a universal sub-second response.

Configured responses can include BGP FlowSpec filtering, remote-triggered blackholing or integration with a diversion workflow. Router capabilities, routing policy and service integrations must support the chosen action. FlowSpec rules can affect legitimate matching traffic; blackholing deliberately drops traffic to the targeted destination, including legitimate users. Threshold tuning and controlled withdrawal are important.

Separate each layer’s responsibility

Agree the operating scope

Ask Axclusive to confirm telemetry coverage, available reports, thresholds, response actions, support hours and pricing for your service. Do not assume every customer receives a dashboard, all response mechanisms or unrestricted use without a defined proposal.

Axclusive’s opt-in DIA Magic Transit service is Always-On: it does not wait for FastNetMon to trigger a diversion before protected routing becomes active. Detection can complement that service, but a specific integration or automated workflow must be agreed and tested.

Common questions

Is FastNetMon the scrubbing network? No. Detection and orchestration are distinct from the routers and upstream services that filter or absorb traffic.

Can automation avoid every false positive? No. Policies need baselines, safeguards and review. Test restoration as well as activation.

Is this a substitute for a WAF? No. Network telemetry and response do not replace application-layer inspection and controls.

Plan detection alongside your connectivity

Share your prefixes, traffic profile and critical services. Compare DDoS protection with your connectivity requirements and read how blackholing affects availability. The proposal should describe the implemented functions and who owns each operational step.

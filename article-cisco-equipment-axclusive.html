Cisco Equipment Solutions for Businesses

Current offering scope

Axclusive supplies equipment, software subscriptions and renewals. Implementation and ongoing Axclusive support are available only within agreed managed-service or business voice solutions. Vendor entitlements are separate from Axclusive operational support.

Explore IT Equipment & Software Supply · Explore managed services

Overview

Axclusive supplies Cisco equipment, licences and renewals for business, enterprise and network customers. Send product models, quantities, required entitlements and delivery or renewal dates for a quotation. Implementation, configuration and ongoing Axclusive support are available only within agreed managed-service or voice solutions; they are not included with standalone equipment purchases.

Challenge

Before working with Axclusive, businesses faced many problems in selecting and deploying network equipment:

  • Not knowing which equipment is suitable for the bandwidth or actual operating model.
  • Product supply and managed operations are different purchases; identify which your organisation requires.
  • Worrying about the high cost of having to use a big brand like Cisco while the needs are not too complicated.
  • Lack of experienced technical team to assess traffic, routing/switching needs and scalability.

Businesses need a partner who is knowledgeable about networks, has the ability to provide genuine Cisco equipment, and is flexible to suggest cost-optimized options when Cisco is not necessary.

Solution

For equipment purchases, confirm the supplied items and vendor entitlements. The implementation activities below apply only where included within an agreed managed-service or voice solution:

Equipment advice based on actual needs

  • Bandwidth assessment, network structure and scalability.
  • Determine when high-end Cisco equipment is needed and when cost-effective equipment can be used.

Provide genuine Cisco equipment

  • Router, switch, firewall according to Cisco standards.
  • Vendor warranty and support entitlements as stated in the product quotation. Axclusive support applies only within an agreed managed-service or voice scope.

Implementation within an agreed managed service

  • Install, configure, test and ensure compatibility with Axclusive's Internet service/infrastructure.
  • Optimize routing, switching, and traffic management for maximum performance.

Operational support within the managed-service agreement

  • Monitor device performance.
  • Adjust configurations as needed.
  • Upgrade advice when the business expands.

This solution helps customers have a stable, secure, budget-friendly network system, and is always ready for growth needs.

Results

The intended benefits of an appropriately scoped managed-service or voice solution include:

  • A stable and suitable network system thanks to choosing the right equipment with the right technical specifications.
  • Optimized costs, because Axclusive only recommends Cisco equipment when really needed and chooses more economical equipment when appropriate.
  • High flexibility can upgrade or expand without changing the entire system.
  • Within an agreed managed service, assign defined configuration and support responsibilities to Axclusive.
  • A single partner for both equipment and connectivity services, making management easy and shortening deployment time.

Product-only purchases cover the quoted items and entitlements. For a managed-service or voice solution, the agreement defines implementation, operating responsibilities and service commitments.

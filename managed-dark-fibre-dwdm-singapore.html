What Axclusive manages

Find the right fibre connection

We check which fibre providers can reach your sites, compare routes and arrange the connection. If you need a backup path, we review available records for sections the routes may share.

Set up capacity for your growth

We select optical equipment to suit your locations, existing systems and capacity needs. We coordinate installation, setup and testing so the parts work together.

Keep one team responsible for network operations

We manage the optical network under an agreed support plan. You know who monitors it, handles faults, coordinates with fibre providers and plans changes or capacity upgrades.

Where MOFN fits

Choose MOFN when you want help building and operating the optical network between your sites. Axclusive coordinates the fibre and equipment as well as the ongoing management, so your team has a clear point of contact.

Connect data centres, ISP network locations or enterprise sites with capacity planned around your applications and future growth.

The network can use DWDM, a technology that carries multiple optical channels over the same fibre. This can provide room to add capacity later, within the limits of the agreed design.

Frequently asked questions

What is Managed Optical Fiber Network (MOFN)?+
MOFN brings the fibre connection, optical equipment, setup and ongoing management into one agreed service. It is for customers who want an optical network planned and operated for them, rather than only buying a connection between two locations.
Can you use our existing fibre or optical equipment?+
Yes, we can assess it. Tell us what you already have and share any route records or equipment details. We’ll explain what can be reused and what may need to change.
Can you arrange a separate backup route?+
We can assess available routes and check whether they share underground ducts, building entrances or other sections, where records allow. We agree the available separation before you order.
What affects pricing and lead time?+
The endpoints, fibre availability, equipment, site access and support requirements all affect the quote and delivery schedule. Share your target date so we can check the dependencies early.

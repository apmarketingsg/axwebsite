Three components. One managed solution.

What is included in MOFN?

Dark fibre, DWDM optical equipment and managed service are all part of the offering. Your proposal sets out the route, capacity, protection option and support commitments.

Included: dark fibre

The dark fibre connection between your sites is included. We check availability, coordinate the fibre provider and arrange protected or unprotected delivery. For protected options, we confirm the available path separation and any shared sections.

Included: DWDM optical equipment

DWDM optical equipment at the connected sites is included, together with installation, configuration and testing. We select equipment around your fibre route, current capacity and growth plans.

Included: managed service

Ongoing management of the optical network is included. Axclusive coordinates monitoring, fault handling, fibre-provider liaison and change planning under the agreed support scope. Support hours, response targets and upgrade arrangements are set out in your proposal.

Choose how you plan for interruptions

Protected or unprotected: the choice is yours.

Both options include dark fibre, DWDM optical equipment and managed service. The difference is the agreed protection arrangement.

Unprotected delivery

A primary fibre path, without a protection path included in the design. Consider this when your wider network already provides backup connectivity, or when a simpler design fits your requirements and budget.

Plan for: how your business will operate while a fibre or equipment fault is being resolved.

Protected delivery

A primary path plus a protection path, with the available switching arrangement agreed in the design. Consider this when continuity is a priority and you need an alternative path as part of the service.

Confirm: route separation, shared equipment or building entries, switching behaviour and acceptance testing.

Protected does not mean every failure is eliminated. We confirm the available routes, what the protection covers and the service commitments before you order.

Where MOFN fits

Choose MOFN when you want help building and operating the optical network between your sites. Axclusive coordinates the fibre and equipment as well as the ongoing management, so your team has a clear point of contact.

Connect data centres, ISP network locations or enterprise sites with capacity planned around your applications and future growth.

The included DWDM equipment carries multiple optical channels over the fibre. This can provide room to add capacity later, within the limits of the agreed design. Additional capacity and future upgrades are scoped separately when required.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose managed dark fibre or a leased line?+
Start with the capacity and operational responsibility you need. A leased line provides agreed private connectivity between endpoints. MOFN combines the dark fibre, DWDM optical equipment and managed service into one solution. Discuss expansion plans and protected or unprotected delivery before choosing the design.
Are dark fibre, DWDM equipment and management all included?+
Yes. These are the three components of Axclusive’s MOFN offering. Your proposal details the fibre route, equipment, installation and managed-service scope, including the chosen protection option and support commitments.
Can I choose protected or unprotected MOFN?+
Yes. Unprotected delivery uses a primary fibre path. Protected delivery adds a protection path with the available switching arrangement agreed in the design. Both include dark fibre, DWDM equipment and managed service. Availability and route separation are confirmed for your locations.
What is Managed Optical Fiber Network (MOFN)?+
MOFN includes dark fibre, DWDM optical equipment, installation and setup, plus ongoing managed service. You can choose protected or unprotected delivery. The proposal confirms the sites, capacity, protection arrangements and support commitments.
Can you use our existing fibre or optical equipment?+
Yes, we can assess it. Tell us what you already have and share any route records or equipment details. We’ll explain what can be reused and what may need to change.
Can you arrange a separate backup route?+
We can assess available routes and check whether they share underground ducts, building entrances or other sections, where records allow. We agree the available separation before you order.
What affects pricing and lead time?+
The endpoints, fibre availability, equipment, site access and support requirements all affect the quote and delivery schedule. Share your target date so we can check the dependencies early.

Ethernet describes a handoff or service presentation. It does not, by itself, identify whether the underlying transport uses dedicated optical channels or packet switching. Confirm the circuit design, committed capacity and service behaviour in the proposal.

What Axclusive manages

Dark fibre procurement

We check which providers can reach your sites, compare available routes and coordinate ordering and handover. Where you need diverse paths, we check available route information for shared segments.

DWDM sourcing and provisioning

We specify and source DWDM equipment based on the fibre route, optical loss, your interfaces and target capacity. We coordinate installation, configuration and testing before handover.

Ongoing network management

We manage the deployed optical network under the agreed support scope. We define monitoring, fault escalation, provider coordination and how changes or capacity upgrades are handled.

Where MOFN fits

MOFN is a managed optical network scope, rather than a synonym for a leased circuit or IPLC. You are asking Axclusive to coordinate the fibre and optical equipment and manage their operation. That network can support a DCI requirement or other site-to-site workloads; its capacity and channels depend on the agreed fibre and DWDM design.

Connect data centres, points of presence, or enterprise sites when you want an optical design sized for your network.

DWDM carries multiple wavelengths over the same fibre pair. That gives you a way to add capacity as demand grows, subject to the fibre route and the equipment’s limits.

Frequently asked questions

What is Managed Optical Fiber Network (MOFN)?+
MOFN is Axclusive’s managed optical networking service. It combines dark fibre sourcing, DWDM equipment and provisioning with ongoing network management under an agreed scope, connecting data centres, points of presence or enterprise sites.
Can you use fibre or DWDM equipment we already have?+
Share your route records, fibre test results and equipment models. We can review what can be reused and what needs to change for the proposed design.
Can you source diverse fibre routes?+
We can assess available routes and check for shared ducts, building entry points and other common segments where route records allow. Confirm the level of diversity before ordering.
What affects pricing and lead time?+
The endpoints, fibre availability, equipment, site access and support requirements all affect the quote and delivery schedule. Share your target date so we can check the dependencies early.

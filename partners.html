Useful answers

Partners FAQ

Do telcos need to apply as a partner?+
No. Telcos, carriers and ISPs can contact Axclusive directly to discuss connectivity and wholesale requirements. The partner programme is targeted at resellers and system integrators.
Who can join the Axclusive partner programme?+
The programme is intended for resellers and system integrators bringing Axclusive services into their customer solutions. Acceptance, commercial terms and responsibilities follow qualification. Telcos, carriers and ISPs can contact Axclusive directly for their network and wholesale requirements.
Can I offer services under my own brand?+
White-label options can be discussed. Branding, customer ownership, support responsibilities and commercial terms must be agreed before offering the service.
Are partner discounts automatic?+
No. Pricing and discounts are discussed after qualification and depend on the approved commercial arrangement. No fixed margin is promised.
How do I apply?+
Use the partner application link or discuss your company, markets, services of interest and customer requirements with Axclusive. The team will confirm the next steps.

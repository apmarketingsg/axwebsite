Privacy Policy

We trust that it will assist you in making an informed decision whether to provide us with any of your Personal Data.

This Policy applies to entities within the Group that is: i) offering services, and ii) collecting, using and/or disclosing Personal Data. As such, obligations set out in this Policy will extend to the relevant entity within the Group that is providing the services to you or is in control and possession of your Personal Data.

For the purposes of understanding this Policy, capitalised terms used in this Policy shall have the following meanings:

“Applicable Laws” means Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 and its subsidiary legislations and regulations as amended from time to time.

“Personal Data” means any data, whether true or not, which is (a) about an individual who can be identified (i) from that data; or (ii) from that data and other information to which we have or are likely to have access and would include data in our records as may be updated from time to time, or (b) defined as “personal data” or “personal information” under Applicable Laws

“Services” means any services offered by the Company, including connectivity, managed, and professional services, together with related value-added services, promotions, and other ancillary offerings, as agreed between you and the Company.

“Website” means the Company website accessed at the following address [https://www.axclusive.com.sg].

We will take reasonable care to ensure that your personal information is accurate, complete, up-to-date and stored in a secure environment, protected from unauthorised access, modification, loss or disclosure.

You agree that we may collect and process data about you, including but not limited to:

  1. Information that you may provide when filling in forms on, or otherwise submitting or making available information to, this Website, or subscribing for the Services with the Company. This includes but is not limited to any information provided when you create a User Account and/or register as a user of this Website or for any services made available by the Company such as your name, telephone number, email address and other contact details;
  2. Any correspondence you may have with us;
  3. Information that may be captured via any error logging and reporting tool that captures error report data and, at your option and with your consent, sends this data to us in order for us to be informed of any software errors or problems that may occur during your use of the Website, the services provided on it and/or the Services;
  4. Details of your visits to our Website, the activities you engage in when using the services made available via the Website, the Services and the resources that you access on or via the Website or the Services; and
  5. Information that may be captured through your cookies and other forms of data analytics applications or services which may be used on this Website.

You must only submit to us information which is accurate and not misleading and you must inform us of changes. You can update your Personal Data anytime by contacting us at our email address below.

  17. To protect the rights, property or safety of any person (including for the purposes of fraud detection and prevention).

  1. Our subsidiaries and other related corporations;
  2. You may request to access and/or correct the Personal Data currently in our possession by writing to us at dpo@axclusive.com.sg. Please note that we may charge you a reasonable fee for the handling and processing of your requests to access your Personal Data.

5. Security of Your Personal Data

We will take reasonable steps to maintain appropriate physical, technical and administrative security to help prevent loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure or modification of your Personal Data. To safeguard your Personal Data, all electronic storage and transmission of Personal Data are secured with appropriate security technologies.

6. Retention of Personal Data

We retain your Personal Data for as long as it is necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, for legal or business purposes of our Company, or as required or allowed by law. We will cease to retain such Personal Data when it is no longer required for the foregoing purposes.

7.1 Cookies are small data files that are sent from a website’s server and are stored on your device’s hard drive. Our website uses “cookies” to collect information to personalise your online experience, which are unique to you.

7.2 We may monitor your use of our website through the use of cookies and similar tracking devices. Information such as the number of times you visit our website, which page(s) and product(s) you view, time spent on those pages, your computer’s internet protocol (IP) address, browser type and other information.

7.3 You may disable cookies via your internet browser software settings. However, some parts of our website may not function as expected if cookies are disabled.
